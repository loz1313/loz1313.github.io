About me

A different perspective. A practical approach.

Be it consulting, writing or mentoring, the common thread has always been people and the possibilities that come from understanding them.

I’ve always been curious about people. What motivates us, how we connect and what we’re capable of achieving.

That curiosity has shaped a career spanning industries, continents and businesses of all sizes.

From Global Head of Operations to Chief Operating Officer, I’ve scaled businesses, managed operations and led major transformations. My experience has taught me to step back and see what matters while understanding the detail that makes things work.

Today, I work with business owners, leaders and individuals to bring clarity, identify opportunities and turn good ideas into momentum. I’m equally comfortable developing strategy as I am rolling up my sleeves to make it happen.

Writing · Memoirs & biographies

Everyone has a story worth telling.

Writing has been my passion all my life, and I wrote my first life story, my grandmother’s, at just 16. Today, I help people bring their stories to life through memoirs, biographies and personal storytelling. Every project begins with listening, and I approach each one with curiosity, empathy and respect for the trust involved, finding the words, structure and voice to tell each story authentically.

Because the stories we leave behind matter.

Mentoring & accountability

When you’re close to a situation, an independent perspective can make all the difference. A life well lived has equipped me with the skills to help. Whether you’re navigating a transition, building something new or facing an important decision, I offer practical mentoring grounded in real-world experience, asking the right questions and pairing honest conversation with accountability to help you move forward with confidence.
